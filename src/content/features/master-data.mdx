## What it does

Master data is the unglamorous foundation. If your fleet table disagrees with your work-order table about an aircraft's registration, your reports are wrong. AMRO Pro keeps the master data normalized and tenant-isolated — every operational record references the master entity, never duplicates its fields.

Technician records track license categories, type ratings, and currency dates. The sign-off enforcement at the work-order level reaches into this data to verify that the named technician actually held the right category on the date the task was completed. No surprises during the audit.
