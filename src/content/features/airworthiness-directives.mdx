## What it does

Airworthiness directives are how regulators say "stop flying until you fix this." Missing one is, in the polite phrasing, "an enforcement event." Most operators track ADs in a single master spreadsheet that one person owns, which is fine until that person is on leave during the compliance window.

AMRO Pro ingests AD bulletins from each authority, evaluates applicability against the fleet's master data (aircraft type, serial range, modification status), and tracks compliance progress through the work orders that close each AD out. Deadlines trigger alerts with configurable lead time. Compliance history is retained forever, with the actual work that achieved compliance linked one click away.
